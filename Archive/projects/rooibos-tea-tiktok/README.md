# AI Short Video Project: 5 Reasons Americans Are Switching to Rooibos Tea

This folder contains a complete TikTok-ready AI short video package for an AI Commerce OS campaign about Rooibos tea, targeted to a US audience.

## Project Structure

```text
projects/rooibos-tea-tiktok/
├── README.md
├── scripts/
│   └── 30-second-video-script.md
├── prompts/
│   └── runway-cinematic-image-prompts.md
├── voiceover/
│   └── voice-over-text.md
├── captions/
│   └── scene-captions.md
├── music/
│   └── background-music-suggestion.md
└── metadata/
    └── tiktok-post.md
```

## How to Use This Project

1. Use `scripts/30-second-video-script.md` as the production blueprint for scene timing, visuals, voice-over, and captions.
2. Copy each prompt from `prompts/runway-cinematic-image-prompts.md` into Runway or another AI video/image generation tool.
3. Record or synthesize the narration from `voiceover/voice-over-text.md`.
4. Add the captions from `captions/scene-captions.md` as on-screen TikTok text.
5. Use the music direction in `music/background-music-suggestion.md` to choose a track that matches the edit.
6. Publish with the title, description, and hashtags in `metadata/tiktok-post.md`.

## Creative Direction

- **Audience:** Health-conscious adults in the United States, especially coffee and black-tea drinkers looking for an evening-friendly alternative.
- **Format:** 30-second vertical TikTok video, 9:16 aspect ratio.
- **Tone:** Warm, modern, premium, educational, and easy to understand.
- **Visual Style:** Cinematic kitchen and lifestyle shots, warm red-gold rooibos color palette, soft morning and evening light, clean US home aesthetic.
- **Call to Action:** Encourage viewers to try rooibos as a naturally caffeine-free daily tea swap.
