# Background Music Suggestion

Use a warm, upbeat, modern wellness lifestyle track with:

- Tempo around **90-105 BPM**.
- Soft lo-fi drums or light organic percussion.
- Warm acoustic guitar, mellow keys, or marimba-style accents.
- Gentle rise from the hook into the five reasons.
- Clean ending hit or soft chime for the final call to action.

## Search Terms for TikTok or Stock Music Libraries

- `warm wellness lifestyle beat`
- `cozy morning tea lo-fi`
- `uplifting organic acoustic pop`
- `modern calm kitchen vlog instrumental`
- `soft feel good wellness background`

## Mood Reference

The track should feel calm but not sleepy: more like a polished wellness brand video than a meditation video.
