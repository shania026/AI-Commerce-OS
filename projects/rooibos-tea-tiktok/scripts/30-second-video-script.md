## Editing Notes

- Keep every cut quick, polished, and movement-based.
- Use warm transitions: steam wipes, pour cuts, and red-gold light leaks.
- Add captions in large, high-contrast white or cream text with subtle shadow.
- Keep the final call to action visible for the last full second.
