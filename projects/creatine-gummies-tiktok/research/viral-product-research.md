# Viral Product Research: Creatine Gummies

## Product chosen

**Creatine monohydrate gummies** are the selected product for the US TikTok short-video campaign.

## Why this product is trending

1. **Supplements are a dominant TikTok Shop health-and-beauty segment.** NutraIngredients reported that vitamins and supplements generated **$784 million** in TikTok Shop sales during the 52 weeks ending February 2026, making supplements TikTok Shop's largest Health & Beauty segment.
2. **Health and beauty is already a major TikTok Shop engine in the US.** NovaData summarized NielsenIQ data showing TikTok Shop became the **#4 US health and beauty retailer** in April 2026, with annual category sales above **$4.4 billion**.
3. **Creatine has moved beyond “gym bro” culture.** Vogue reported in February 2026 that creatine has been reframed as an “it-girl” supplement, with newer gummies, chews, and sachets aimed at women, older adults, and everyday wellness consumers.
4. **Gummy delivery makes the product TikTok-friendly.** Men's Health described the broader rise of “candyceuticals,” noting consumer interest in sweet, nostalgic, easy-to-take gummy supplements among Millennial and Gen Z audiences.
5. **Creatine gummies solve a visible pain point.** NY Post coverage of viral creatine gummies highlighted consumer interest in avoiding gritty powders and chalky aftertaste, while emphasizing portability, fruity flavors, and stackable dosing.
6. **Current health conversations are still active.** Recent coverage from July 2026 discussed creatine's established sports-nutrition role and on-trend gummy formats, while also cautioning that quality, verified testing, and proper dosing matter.

## Why it has the highest TikTok viral potential

Creatine gummies combine three TikTok-friendly traits:

- **Visual contrast:** messy scoop and shaker vs. colorful gummies.
- **Routine content:** gym bag, desk drawer, car console, and “what I take before lifting” formats.
- **Shareable misconception hook:** “Creatine is not just for gym bros anymore” creates debate without needing unsafe medical claims.

## Compliance and safety notes

- Avoid disease-treatment claims.
- Avoid promising weight loss, cognitive enhancement, hormone changes, or guaranteed muscle gain.
- Say “supports strength/recovery routines when paired with training” rather than “builds muscle fast.”
- Encourage viewers to check serving size, third-party testing, ingredients, and to consult a healthcare professional if pregnant, nursing, under 18, taking medication, or managing a medical condition.
