# TikTok Short Video Project: Creatine Gummies

**Target market:** United States  
**Chosen product:** Creatine monohydrate gummies  
**Video length:** 30 seconds  
**Positioning:** A convenient, visually satisfying wellness supplement format for active adults who dislike gritty powder.

## Project files

- `research/viral-product-research.md` — trend rationale, product selection, compliance notes, and source links.
- `scripts/30-second-video-script.md` — timed 30-second TikTok script with the hook in the first 3 seconds.
- `storyboard/scene-by-scene-storyboard.md` — shot-by-shot plan.
- `prompts/runway-image-prompts.md` — Runway image prompts for every scene.
- `voiceover/voice-over.md` — final voice-over copy.
- `captions/subtitle-text.md` — subtitle text by scene.
- `metadata/tiktok-post.md` — TikTok title, description, and 10 hashtags.

## Creative angle

The video uses a quick “powder vs gummy” contrast: messy scoop, gritty shaker, then a clean, portable gummy routine. It avoids medical promises and frames the product as a convenience-forward supplement format.
